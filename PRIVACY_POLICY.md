# Privacy Policy — All Currency Converter

**Last updated: 24 September 2026**

All Currency Converter ("the app") is an Android currency converter published by
Ismail Idris. This policy describes exactly what the app does with information.
It is written against the app's actual behaviour, not a template.

## Short version

The app has no accounts and no sign-in. Everything you enter — the currencies
you pick, your settings, and any rate you type in yourself — stays on your
phone. The app sends no personal information anywhere. It does show ads from
Google AdMob, and those ads work the way ads on Android normally do.

## What stays on your device

The app keeps a small database on your phone containing:

- the currencies shown on your converter screen,
- your settings (theme, automatic refresh, thousand separators, vibration, and
  the naira adjustment amount),
- a count of how often you pick each currency, so the ones you use appear first,
- any exchange rate you enter yourself ("own rates"),
- the most recently downloaded exchange rates, so the app works offline,
- a count of how many ads have been shown today, so the app can limit them.

None of this is uploaded, shared, or visible to the developer. It is removed
when you uninstall the app or clear its data from Android settings. Android's
automatic backup is disabled for this app, so this data is not copied to your
Google account either.

## What leaves your device

**Exchange rates.** To update rates the app requests public, read-only data
from:

- ExchangeRate-API — `https://open.er-api.com`
- Frankfurter — `https://api.frankfurter.app` (used if the first is unavailable)

These requests ask only "what are the current rates for US dollars". They carry
no information about you, what you are converting, or what amounts you type.
Like any internet request, they do reveal your device's IP address to those
services, which is how the internet works rather than something the app sends
on purpose. Their handling of that is covered by their own policies.

**Advertising.** The app displays ads through Google AdMob. To serve ads,
Google's advertising software collects information from the device, which may
include your advertising ID, IP address, device model and operating system
version, and general (non-precise) location derived from the IP address. This
happens inside Google's software; the app itself does not collect, see, or
store any of it. Google's own privacy policy governs that data:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

Google's information about how it uses data in advertising is here:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## Your choices about ads

- **Consent.** Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic
  Area and the United Kingdom), the app asks for your choice through Google's
  consent form before requesting ads, and it re-asks when that choice expires.
- **Advertising ID.** You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in
  **Settings → Privacy → Ads** on your Android device. Deleting it stops
  personalised advertising.
- **Ad frequency.** The app deliberately limits full-screen ads to at most three
  per day, never more than one per session, and never while you are in the
  middle of a calculation.

## What the app does not do

- It has no account, login, or user profile.
- It does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or contacts.
- It does not use analytics or crash-reporting services.
- It does not request location permission and does not use GPS.
- It does not track you across other apps or websites.
- It does not sell data to anyone, because it does not collect any.
- The only Android permissions it requests are internet access and vibration.

## About the rates shown

The app is a converter, not a currency exchange and not a price service. Rates
come from the public sources named above, are indicative, and may be out of date
or wrong. Where the app shows a naira figure, it is derived from the official
published rate with a fixed amount added, which you can view and change in
**Settings → Rates**. Rates you enter yourself are your own figures. Nothing in
the app is an offer to buy or sell currency, and no rate shown should be relied
on for a transaction.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect
information from them.

## Changes to this policy

If the app's behaviour changes, this page changes with it, and the date at the
top is updated.
